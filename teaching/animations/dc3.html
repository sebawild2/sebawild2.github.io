DC3 / Skew Algorithm

Linear-time suffix sorting. 1. Rank the suffixes Ti with i ≢ 0 (mod 3) recursively, by turning triples of characters into single letters. 2. Induce the ranks of the suffixes with i ≡ 0 from them. 3. Merge the two sorted lists, using the ranks to compare in constant time.
