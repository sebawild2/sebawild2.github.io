The turnpike problem  ·  puzzles

A toll road collects a toll between every pair of its exits, and the toll is simply the distance driven. The first tab shows where the tolls come from; the second one turns the question around: given only the multiset D of all pairwise tolls, where are the exits? Place them so that the road realizes exactly these tolls — the comparison on the right updates live, and turns green when you have solved it.
